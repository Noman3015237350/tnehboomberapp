// WHO WE ARE

About Us

TNEH Ethical Hacker Group BD is an independent cybersecurity community focused on sharing knowledge, encouraging responsible security research, and promoting cybersecurity awareness across Bangladesh and beyond.

We believe cybersecurity is essential for protecting individuals, organizations, and digital infrastructure. Through education and collaboration, we aim to help members develop practical skills and a strong understanding of defensive security concepts.

Whether you are just starting your journey in cybersecurity or you are an experienced professional, TNEH provides a supportive space to learn, share, and grow together while maintaining the highest ethical and legal standards.
